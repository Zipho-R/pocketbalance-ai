PocketBalance AI for Smarter Money Decisions

A financial insight experience that goes beyond tracking numbers by interpreting spending behaviour, identifying pressure points, and explaining budget health in a more human, transparent way.

PocketBalance AI helps users understand where their money goes, compare their spending against an income-based budget model, and receive recommendations that feel practical, explainable, and easier to act on.

Built around adaptive budgeting models such as 50/30/20 and other income-based frameworks, so users are evaluated against a model that better fits their financial reality.
